About HealthRisk AI

Empowering preventive healthcare through artificial intelligence

Our Mission

HealthRisk AI is designed to help individuals understand their health risk profile using machine learning models trained on comprehensive medical and lifestyle data. We aim to promote early detection and preventive care through accessible technology.

Technology

Our platform leverages Python, Flask, Scikit-learn, and SQLite to deliver a robust full-stack solution. We compare seven machine learning algorithms and automatically select the best performer based on accuracy, F1 score, and ROC AUC metrics.

System Architecture

Data Layer
SQLite + CSV Dataset
ML Pipeline
Preprocessing + Training
Backend
Flask + SQLAlchemy
Frontend
Bootstrap 5 + Chart.js
